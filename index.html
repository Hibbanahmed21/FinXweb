FRIDAY’s AI Model Outperforms Chat GPT, Claude, Gemini, and Llama

Generative AI platforms, such as Chat GPT and Claude, are great at answering questions on a wide range of subjects. FRIDAY was trained to go deep into personal finance and understands the complex relationships between people and their money. As result, FRIDAY scores significantly higher than all other AI models at the Certified Financial Planner® exam.

The Certified Financial Planner® exam consists of 170 multiple-choice and short-answer questions and typically takes 6 ours to complete. It covers several personal finance topics, such as financial planning principles, investments, retirement planning, tax planning, and more.

Measure Financial Wellness with FRIDAY’s Financial Health Index

To determine if FRIDAY can help you, we needed a way to measure overall financial health. Our financial experts have developed the Financial Health Index (FHI), a comprehensive metric to evaluate your overall financial well-being on a 0–100 scale. By analyzing factors such as income, expenses, savings, investments, debt, and net worth, the FHI offers a holistic view of your financial status. Integrated into the FRIDAY platform, this dynamic score allows you to monitor and improve your financial health over time, empowering you to make informed decisions and achieve your financial goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does FRIDAY help me manage my finances?

FRIDAY analyzes your financial habits and creates a custom plan to help you reach your goals, whether it’s paying off debt or saving for a big purchase.

Do I have to link my bank accounts?

No worries! You can use FRIDAY by manually entering your estimated income, expenses, debts, and investments. We’ll provide you with solid financial advice either way. But if you choose to connect your bank accounts, FRIDAY can offer even more accurate and personalized plans.

How is my data protected?

Your privacy and security are our top priorities. FRIDAY uses advanced encryption and strict privacy protocols to safeguard your data. We adhere to industry-standard cybersecurity practices, ensuring your information is stored securely and never shared without your explicit consent.

What goals can FRIDAY help with?

FRIDAY is here to help you achieve a wide range of financial goals, from saving for a dream vacation or a new car to reducing debt and planning for retirement. We tailor our advice specifically to your personal financial situation and aspirations.

Is FRIDAY right for financial beginners?

Absolutely! FRIDAY is designed to be user-friendly and perfect for beginners of any age. We offer straightforward advice, easy-to-understand insights, and educational resources to help you grow your financial knowledge and confidence.

Is FRIDAY expensive to use?

Not at all. FRIDAY offers a free basic version with all the essential features you need to get started. For more advanced functionalities, including personalized advice and comprehensive analytics, we offer a premium subscription at a price that’s less than your monthly coffee or beer budget. Plus, FRIDAY will let you know if we can’t help you save money!
