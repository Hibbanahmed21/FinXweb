## 🎯 **How to Test:**

### **Step 1: Open in Browser**
- Copy and paste: `http://localhost:8001/chat.html`
- Should see dark theme with purple accent colors
- Sidebar on left, main chat area on right

### **Step 2: Try General Query**
1. Click "💬 General Query" button
2. Type: "How should I budget my salary?"
3. Press Enter or click Send
4. Watch typing indicator appear
5. See AI response with bullet points

### **Step 3: Try Personal Query** 
1. Click "＋ New" to start fresh
2. Click "👤 Personal Query" 
3. Choose category: "Budgeting & Saving"
4. Choose subcategory: "Monthly Budget"
5. Fill the intake form with sample data
6. Click "Continue"
7. See personalized setup message
8. Try the suggested starter buttons

### **Step 4: Test Features**
- Click ☰ to collapse/expand sidebar
- Create multiple chats and switch between them
- Try on mobile by resizing browser window
- Use Ctrl+K to search, Ctrl+N for new chat
